# Better Music Toast

Shows the song that's currently playing in a small box that fits right into the Minecraft HUD.

**Download:** [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/better-music-toast) · [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/better-music-toast)

## Features

- A small toast with the song title and artist whenever a new track starts, including menu music and music discs
- Colour themes, several positions and sizes, shown for the whole song or just a few seconds
- Moves out of the way when advancements, the boss bar or other notifications show up
- Classic mode: only C418's soundtrack, with the tracks that belong to each biome
- Music Frequency and music that keeps playing while the game is paused (both from Minecraft 1.21.6) on every
  older version, back to 1.8.9
- Option to hide Minecraft's own music toast

Settings: Options → Music & Sounds → **Better Music Toast**, or through Mod Menu (Fabric) or the Mods list
(NeoForge, Forge).

## Supported versions

- **Fabric:** Minecraft 1.16 – 1.16.5 and 1.18 – 26.3, with [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api);
  [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) optional
- **NeoForge:** Minecraft 1.20.1 – 26.3
- **Forge:** Minecraft 1.8.9, 1.9.4, 1.10.2, 1.11 – 1.11.2, 1.12 – 1.12.2, 1.16.1 – 1.16.5, 1.18 – 1.19.4 and 1.20 – 26.3 (on 1.16.1 Forge 32.0.72 or newer,
  on 1.18.2 Forge 40.1.60 or newer)

Client-side only, not needed on servers. Built with [Stonecutter](https://stonecutter.kikugie.dev/) from one
codebase for all these versions.
