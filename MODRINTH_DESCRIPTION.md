Shows the name of the song that's currently playing, in a small box that fits the Minecraft HUD.

I play a lot of singleplayer and always wanted to know which track is on, especially with all the newer songs I don't recognize yet. The mods I found only flashed colorful text above the hotbar for a couple of seconds, which I didn't like. So I made my own, where you decide where it goes and how long it stays.

## What it does

When a new track starts, a small beige box with a brown border pops up with the song title and the composer, for example "Aerie" by Lena Raine. The names come straight from the game, so every vanilla track shows up correctly, including menu music and music discs.

## Settings

You can put it in a corner, at the top center or above the hotbar, and choose if it stays for the whole song or just a few seconds. It also works with music discs and can replace Minecraft's own music popup.

If an advancement or something else pops up in the same spot, the box moves out of the way or hides for a moment, so nothing gets covered.

## Where to find the settings

- Options → Music & Sounds → **Better Music Toast**
- or through Mod Menu, if you have it
- There are also two keybinds under Controls (show the current song again, open settings). They're unbound by default.

## Good to know

- Client-side only. You don't need it on a server.
- Requires Fabric API. Mod Menu is optional.
- Available in English and German.
- Music from other mods should work too. It then shows the file name and the mod's name.
- I use it together with Sodium, Iris, Lithium and a bunch of other mods in my own pack without problems. If something breaks for you, let me know.
