# Changelog

All versions of Better Music Toast (called "Now Playing Toast" before 1.2.1), newest first.

## 1.6.8
- New: "Note Animation" setting (on by default). Turn it off for music notes that stand still. It sits next to "On Notifications", which is now a half-width button.
- Fixed: When creating or loading a world, the display kept showing the menu song although the music had already stopped.
- Fixed: With several notifications in a row, the display could briefly end up over them. It now stays below them the whole time and only moves back once the spot has been free for a moment. If there is no room left beside the notifications, it steps aside completely.

## 1.6.7
- Changed: Better Music Toast now has its own license ("All Rights Reserved"). Playing it, reading its code, showing it in videos and putting it in any modpack are allowed without asking. Re-uploading the mod or publishing modified versions needs permission. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- Released for every supported Minecraft version at once. No gameplay changes.
- New (added later in 1.6.7): Forge for Minecraft 1.20 and 1.20.2 – 26.3, NeoForge for Minecraft 1.20.2 – 1.20.5.

## 1.6.6
- New: Minecraft 1.16 – 1.16.5 on Fabric and Forge (Forge from 1.16.1), running on Java 8 like Minecraft 1.16 itself.
- New: Minecraft 1.18 – 1.18.2 on Fabric and Forge.
- Changed: In Music & Sounds on 1.16 and 1.18, "Show Subtitles" moved next to "Voice/Speech", so the mod's buttons fit neatly below "Device".
- Fixed: Fabric 1.19 – 1.19.2 did not detect Fabric API on some versions and refused to start.

## 1.6.5
- New: Minecraft 1.19 – 1.19.4 on Fabric and Forge.
- Fixed: A crash at the end of a song on Minecraft 1.19 – 1.20.4 (Minecraft's music decoder used more and more memory after the end of a song).

## 1.6.4
- New: Minecraft 1.20 – 1.20.6 on Fabric, Forge 1.20.1 (also runs on NeoForge 1.20.1) and NeoForge 1.20.6.
- New: Minecraft 1.8.9 on Forge, with Music Frequency and music that keeps playing while the game is paused.
- Changed: "Size" is now a button that cycles through the sizes.

## 1.6.3
- New: NeoForge support for Minecraft 1.21 – 26.3.
- Fixed: A crash on Minecraft 26.3.

## 1.6.2
- New: Minecraft 1.21 – 1.21.4.
- Fixed: Song names with dashes (" - ", " – ", " — ") are split into artist and title correctly.
- Fixed: Hover highlighting of the themed buttons.

## 1.6.1

- New: One mod for many Minecraft versions: Fabric 26.1, 1.21.11, 1.21.9 – 1.21.10, 1.21.6 – 1.21.8 and 1.21.5. Versions before 1.21.6 also get Music Frequency and music that keeps playing while the game is paused.

- New: Live preview of the box in the settings.
